Kabıl aluu / Admission 2026

Documents are taken from 15 June to 20 August 2026 in room 2.

  1. Application to the director
  2. Certificate of 9 or 12 grades (original)
  3. For grade 12 graduates: the Graduation Test result sheet
  4. Medical certificate 086-u
  5. 4 photos 3x4
  6. Copy of passport

Grant places are given by the average mark of the certificate, or for grade 12 graduates by the Test points in Iradi language and literature. Fee for contract places: 9,500 som a year. Hostel: 60 places for girls, 20 for boys.

Graduates who sign the contract to work 3 years in a district school study on a grant place and receive the housing allowance there.
