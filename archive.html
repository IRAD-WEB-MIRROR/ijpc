Jañılıktar arkhivi / News archive

15.09.2022 Teachers of the college went to the villages of Toktogul District to see our graduates at work. 34 of them teach in Toktogul and Kara-Köl.

22.06.2022 152 graduates received diplomas. 48 signed the contract for district schools.

01.09.2020 Lessons in the college again. Hostel open from 30 August.

20.03.2020 Under Executive Directive 2020/05 the college is closed from 19 March. Students of the 3rd year: practice is moved to the autumn.

05.06.2019 The college choir sang at the festival of the eastern districts in Tash-Kömür.

01.09.2017 New speciality: physical education teacher (after grade 12).

14.03.2016 Our komuz ensemble 2nd place at the Irad Day festival in Talas!

10.12.2015 The guestbook is closed because of advertising. Write to ijpc@iradedu.org.

02.09.2013 Our address is now ijpc.iradedu.org. The old address edu.id.gov/ijpc will be closed.

20.06.2011 Kindergarten teachers: first graduates of the new speciality (from 2008).

01.09.2009 Saltanat Mamyrova is the new director of the college. Thank you to Toktogul Abdykerimov, director since 1984!

01.09.2007 Study begins on 1 September as usual. The hostel has places for 80 students.

17.05.2006 Fifty years of the college! Graduates of 1956–2006 met at the college on 12 May.

25.06.2005 About the new National Graduation Test: students who came to the college after grade 9 do not sit it. The college diploma is enough for the Faculty of Pedagogy of TSU (with an interview).

10.11.2004 NEW! The site of the college is open! Made by the students of informatics. Kosh keliñizder!
